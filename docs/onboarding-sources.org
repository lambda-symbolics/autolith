* Welcome to Autolith
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: welcome
:VERSION: 3
:END:
[[https://autolith.rocks][@@brand:Autolith@@]] is an agent you can inspect and change while it runs.

I'm [[https://twitter.com/LukasHozda][*Lukáš Hozda*]]. I made this tour to help you get started.

You'll try the prompt, then explore what you can do with Autolith.

@@dim:Press Space to continue, or Esc to skip. Replay anytime with (onboard).@@

* Adapt the system while it runs
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: live-image
:VERSION: 4
:END:
Autolith runs inside a *live Common Lisp image*. You and the agent can inspect, define, test, and revise behavior without a rebuild.

@@brand:inspect -> redefine -> test -> private commit@@

Autolith records persistent self-modifications in a journal and replays them from private image commits.

Run =autolith --immutable= to prevent self-modification during a session.

* Connect your first provider
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: first-provider
:VERSION: 2
:EXERCISE: authentication
:END:
Choose a provider and sign in before your first conversation.

For a *ChatGPT subscription*, choose *chatgpt*. Use *browser* for OAuth with a local callback, or *device-code* to enter the displayed code in your browser.

For an *OpenAI API key*, choose *openai-api* and paste your key at the hidden prompt. Autolith saves it in its private credential store and discovers your available models. Follow the login or API-key prompts for other providers.

Don't want to sign in yet? Type =continue=.

Press *Esc* in the method picker to return to providers, or in the provider picker to return here. You can retry after a cancelled or failed login.

@@brand:PRACTICE  Type (auth) to sign in, or continue to skip.@@

* Compose requests with Lisp
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: prompt-function
:VERSION: 4
:END:
Use =(prompt ...)= to send a request built in Lisp. Compute the text, read it from a file, or include images.

=(prompt "Review this change")=

Use =:to= to send a request to a named child that is already running:

* Stay in control while work is active
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: steering
:VERSION: 3
:END:
@@brand:NOW   Enter  -> steer the active turn

*Press Enter to steer the current work.* Autolith reads all pending steers together at the next safe point.

*Press Tab to queue a follow-up.* Autolith handles follow-ups one at a time, in order, after the current work ends.

* Leave the terminal.  Keep the session.
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: detached-sessions
:VERSION: 5
:END:
Use =(detach)= to disconnect your terminal while Autolith keeps working.

*From another terminal:*
=autolith localgroup status=
=autolith localgroup attach [SESSION-ID]=
=autolith localgroup tell   [SESSION-ID] "message"=
=autolith localgroup pause  [SESSION-ID]=
=autolith localgroup detach [SESSION-ID]=
=autolith localgroup kill   [SESSION-ID]=

To watch without sending input, use =attach --read-only=.
To take over the session, use =attach --take-over=.
Use =autolith lg= as shorthand for =autolith localgroup=.
With one active session, omit =SESSION-ID=.
With several, use an ID from =status= or a unique prefix of 1+ characters.

* Give Autolith an objective
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: goals
:VERSION: 3
:END:
Use a request for the next task. Set a *goal* for longer autonomous work.

=(goal "Make the project ready for release")=
=(goal :pause)=   pause autonomous work
=(goal :resume)=  resume it
=(goal :clear)=   remove the objective

For explicit acceptance criteria, verification gates, and budgets, use =(mission ...)=.

* Set acceptance criteria and a budget
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: missions
:VERSION: 1
:END:
Use a *mission* when you need explicit acceptance criteria and limits on turns, tokens, and time.

=(mission :start
 '(:objective "Prepare a release summary"
   :turn-limit 20 :token-limit 50000 :wall-seconds 1800
   :criteria ((:id "review" :description "I accept the summary"))))=

=(mission :run)=     begin work
=(mission :status)=  inspect progress, budgets, and evidence
=(mission :resume)=  continue after a pause or resolved blocker

To finish this example, inspect the result, then record your acceptance:
=(mission :accept "review" "I checked and accept the summary")=
=(mission :verify)=

For automated checks, link criteria to verification gates. To finish a mission, pass all criteria and gates.
Use =(mission :cancel "Reason")= to stop instead.

* Keep evidence with what you remember
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: symbolic-evidence
:VERSION: 1
:END:
Autolith keeps *prose memories* and *structured facts* with their sources. A fact can have several independent supports.

=(memories)=           browse memories and facts
=(memories "deploy")=  filter by topic
=(why "ID")=           inspect sources and dependencies

Check stale or invalid entries before relying on them: a cited file may have changed, a premise may no longer hold, or the evidence may have expired.

@@brand:read the evidence -> record it -> recheck when it changes@@

* Follow facts through rules
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: symbolic-rules
:VERSION: 1
:END:
An admitted *rule* derives facts locally. Move a meeting and its calculated departure time changes without a model request.

=(rules)=                   inspect versions and admission
=(admit-rule "departure")=  approve a proposed rule
=(what-changed)=            inspect changes and their causes

Use =(why "ID")= to inspect a conclusion's premises. A conclusion based on an assumption is still an assumption. Conflicting facts are listed together; missing evidence means unknown.

@@dim:The next-turn notice counts recalled facts and reports changes to facts used earlier.@@

* Check plans and calls against facts
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: symbolic-actions
:VERSION: 1
:END:
*Plan steps* can require facts and use their values in step text. Unsatisfied requirements appear as blocked. If evidence for a completed step changes, inspect the warning before continuing.

*Mission fact gates* check accepted evidence. *Action contracts* check requirements before a tool call and describe its predicted effects and observed results.

Before admitting a contract, review its version, its tool-result-to-fact mappings, and its evidence policy. Admit each changed version separately.

=(mission :status)=  inspect gates and evidence

* Reuse a learned sequence
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: symbolic-procedures
:VERSION: 1
:END:
Autolith can learn a *parameterized procedure* locally after repeating the same tool-call sequence in three successful turns.

=(procedures)=  inspect calls, admission, runs, and measured savings

When one fits your request, you'll see a notice. Its first run needs admission; each call also needs its usual approval and contract checks.

Automatic reuse requires successful runs of the *exact admitted version* with measured savings in both model requests and tokens.

Use =(retire-procedure "NAME")= to stop using and learning that procedure.

* Diagnose before repairing
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: symbolic-diagnosis
:VERSION: 2
:END:
When several causes could explain a failure, Autolith can check known facts and run *diagnostic probes* before choosing a repair.
Read the results and suggested repair in the diagnosis report.
Cases without a known cause are marked open.

Probes are on by default. Turn them off in =(settings)=.

=(diagnoses)=  inspect saved diagnoses and recent cases

@@dim:Structured knowledge must be active. Each probe needs its ordinary tool approval and must satisfy its action contract.@@
