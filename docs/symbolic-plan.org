#+TITLE: Structured knowledge, procedures, and diagnosis
#+STARTUP: overview

This records the symbolic capabilities built on the =lho/symbolic-autolith=
branch: what they are, what changed for users, and what is still open. Its
first use is the release notes. Every item under [[*Release notes][Release

* Release notes

Not shipped yet.

** Memories with sources
- Memories record what they were written from: quoted file text, web pages,
  your own words, or other memories they follow from. A memory whose quoted
  file text or premise changed is shown as stale, and one whose evidence
  cannot be checked as unverified, in recall and in its notice:
  =@ 2 relevant memories exist · 1 stale=.
- =(memories)= lists memories with their status, =(memory "ID")= explains one
  with its sources, and =(forget "ID")= removes one.
- Replacing a memory without sources keeps the recorded ones, so a stale
  memory stays stale until it is checked against current sources.
- A workspace export includes the global memories its memories rely on.

** Facts and rules
- Besides prose memories, Autolith keeps structured facts: typed values with
  units, dates, instants, and durations, each citing its evidence and why it
  is believed (an observation, your statement, an assumption, or the model's
  own proposal). The basis comes from where the evidence was checked, never
  from the model's label: retrieve a web source with =web_extra.gist= to
  record its URL, result identity, and observation time. Web pages expire a
  week after retrieval and tool output after a day; retrieve them again to
  renew their evidence.
- Rules derive facts from facts, locally and without a model request: move a
  meeting and the departure time moves with it. Conflicting facts are kept and
  shown, never silently resolved. A rule the model proposes derives nothing
  until it is admitted through the usual approval or =(admit-rule)=.
- Recall offers current facts complete, and =@ 1 fact given earlier changed=
  explains a change to the model once.
- New commands: =(why "ID")=, =(what-changed)=, =(rules)=, =(admit-rule)=,
  =(retire-rule)=; =(memories)= lists facts too, conflicted and no-longer
  holding ones first, and =(forget)= retracts a fact.
- New =memory:= resources for the model: =memory:facts=, =memory:claim/ID=,
  =memory:predicates=, =memory:rules=, =memory:methods=, =memory:contracts=,
  =memory:procedures=, =memory:changes=.
- New setting =Structured knowledge= (=knowledge-mode=: =active=, =passive=,
  =off=; =AUTOLITH_KNOWLEDGE=). =passive= and =off= leave model requests
  exactly as without it.
- Mission status and gate results are recorded as facts.
- Facts, rules, and procedures travel in data exports; a relocated workspace
  keeps them.

** Plans, methods, gates, and contracts
- Plan steps can require facts and read their values (=Leave at {?leave}=);
  a step is shown blocked when nothing satisfies it, and a done step whose
  facts stop holding is flagged. =@ 1 plan step changed with its facts=.
- =plan.expand= turns a task into a checked plan with task methods, locally.
- Mission gates of kind =:fact= pass only on undisputed observed facts or
  your statements, never on the model's predictions; a derived fact counts
  only if all it rests on, negated premises included, is accepted.
- Action contracts: admit the exact observation mapping and acceptance policy
  through the usual approval. Requirements use accepted, undisputed facts;
  successful results supply the admitted observations. A replacement needs
  fresh admission; the previous admitted policy applies until then.

** Learned procedures
- When three turns make the same tool calls, Autolith learns a procedure from
  them, locally: =@ learned procedure make-sales-report from 3 turns=.
- A request that fits is offered the one =procedure.run= call that does the
  work; the first run asks to admit it. Completed, successful model turns of
  that exact version must demonstrate both request and token savings before
  a matching request runs it before the model's first request. Procedures
  are learned only from turns the model finished.
- Every call keeps its own approval and contracts; a failed call hands the
  work back to the model, and two failed runs in a row suspend it.
- New commands: =(procedures)= with each procedure's measured savings,
  =(run-procedure)=, =(admit-procedure)=, =(retire-procedure)=.

** Diagnosis
- New setting =Diagnostic probes= (=diagnosis-p=, =AUTOLITH_DIAGNOSIS=), off by
  default. With it, =diagnosis.run= finds which of up to four hypotheses
  explains a failure by running the probes that tell them apart, at most
  three, before any repair. A case no probe settles is reported as open.
- =(diagnoses)= lists saved diagnoses and recent cases with the facts that
  decided them.

** Storage and compatibility
- Structured knowledge lives in a new append-only log, =knowledge.sexp=, next
  to =memories.sexp=. Older generations never read it, so rolling back keeps
  every memory readable.

* Design

** Storage
The knowledge log is append-only: a batch of records is one =:transaction=
form, so an interrupted write leaves none of it, and a reader folds only what
was appended since its cached read. Unknown record kinds are skipped, so a
log written by a newer generation stays readable. Record kinds: schemas,
evidence, claim supports, retractions, rules and their admission, methods,
contracts, procedures with admission, retirement and runs, diagnoses and
diagnosis cases.

Derivations, statuses, and conflicts are evaluated from the log, the cited
files, and the time, and never stored. Evaluated views are reused until the
log, a cited file, or a validity boundary changes.

** Model
- Terms: strings, rationals, =:true= / =:false=, =(:instant)=, =(:date)=,
  =(:duration SECONDS)=, =(:quantity MAGNITUDE "unit")= with unit dimensions.
- Predicate schemas name typed arguments and the key positions; supported
  claims that agree on the key and differ elsewhere conflict.
- A claim's identity is the digest of scope, workspace, and proposition, so
  equal propositions share one claim with several supports.
- Supports carry a basis: =:observation=, =:user-assertion=, =:assumption=,
  =:model-proposal=. Derivation never upgrades a basis.
- Rules are typed, range-restricted, and acyclic; negated premises match only
  explicit negations, never missing facts. Evaluation is capped and reports
  what it could not decide as unknown, never false.

** Procedures
Procedures are data: a sequence of tool calls whose arguments are constants,
parameters, text around a parameter, or values taken from an earlier call's
JSON result. They are learned by anti-unification of at least three accepted
traces with the same tool sequence, kept only when leave-one-out reproduces
every source turn, and run through the ordinary tool registry. Traces are
read from conversation logs and cached in memory, never written elsewhere.

** Diagnosis
Hypotheses predict facts; probes are tool calls whose contracts observe facts.
Probes are ranked by the pairs of remaining hypotheses they tell apart per
cost. A hypothesis is eliminated only by a fact contradicting a prediction.

* Deviations from the original plan

- Procedures are replayed through the tool registry instead of being compiled
  into executable Skills: each step keeps its normal approval and contracts,
  instead of a Skill needing full access on every run.
- Procedures are admitted through the usual approval or =(admit-procedure)=,
  not =refinement-assess=.
- Neural generalization keeps the plan's default allowance of zero; nothing
  in this work calls a model to learn.

* Measurements

Fixture and benchmark results, not live results:
- 10,002 claims and 50,010 supports (29 MB log): cold read 0.7 s, evaluation
  about 0.2 s, view reuse free, an assertion about 8 ms, heap about 145 MB.
- Ten recurring reports with a scripted model, learning included: 40 model
  requests down to 20, and tokens 562,000 down to 173,600 under the fixture's
  token model.
- Learning over 43 MB of conversations: 0.24 s cold, 2 ms after a new turn.
- Remote-service diagnosis: each of three causes found with one or two probes
  and fixed with one repair, against six repairs tried in turn.
