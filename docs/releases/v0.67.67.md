# Autolith 0.67.67

## Commands and settings

- Remove slash commands and their argument parsers.
- Remove `autolith replay` and `autolith tool-failures`.
- Remove the `autolith --update` alias.
- Remove plain terminal mode and `--plain`.
- Remove the top-level `--detached`, `--image`, `-i`, and `--almighty` flags.
- Remove `(btw)`, `(side)`, `(fast)`, `(tasks)`, and the `(close)` alias.
- Remove Relax mode and its prompt warning.
- Remove `(trace)`, `(timestamps)`, `(titles)`, `(cache-misses)`, and `(compact-tool)`.
- Remove the transcript-style selector and REPL-only transcript navigation.
- Remove the compact transcript view setting.
- Remove the activity line and its onboarding choice.
- Remove slash-command aliases and the slash form from saved command records.
- Remove slash text from client command invocation.
- Remove the Management REPL enable switch and its buffer, timeout, queue, and client-limit settings.
- Remove the settings for telemetry buffering, telemetry deadlines, and startup tip rotation.
- Remove `(reset-contracts)` and `rlm.distill`.

## Agents, channels, and missions

- Remove the separate identity for an agent and its channel.
- Remove agent parking and live context transfer between channels.
- Remove `(move)`, `(dismiss)`, and `(domestic)`.
- Remove the standalone goal runner and its dedicated tests. `(goal)` now runs a mission without a budget.
- Remove batch spawning and the `async` and `blocking` options from `child.spawn`.
- Remove the blocking setting from child roles.
- Remove required output schemas from child roles and headless jobs.
- Remove structured yield records from child results and mission reviews.
- Remove the built-in engineering role and its artifact format.
- Remove `child.reset`, `yield.submit`, and `yield.note`.
- Remove approval and ownership checks on shared shell-log reads and searches.
- Remove restrictions on reading and searching another channel’s history in the same world.
- Remove the producer-only restriction on stored context reads.
- Remove parentage checks on shared-memory writes.
- Remove tool restrictions based on who spawned an agent.
- Give the shell permission classifier the agent’s assignment as context, not as approval.
- Fix missing command rows in nested channels when the agent cannot spawn children.
- Remove duplicate client logic for job progress, settings, and permissions.
