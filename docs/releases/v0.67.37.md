# 0.67.37

## Context accounting

Count request-local guidance once when reusing a measured context sample. The context meter, compaction decisions and request admission share the same arithmetic. Replacing, shrinking or removing temporary guidance adjusts its previous contribution instead of adding it again. Durable samples retain the bounded accounting data needed after reload.

## Channel loading

Fullscreen startup and channel switches now show a centered loading bar with actual milestones: connecting to the world server, selecting a channel, reading history, initializing the provider and tools, starting the channel and attaching the terminal. Reusing a loaded channel skips history loading and initialization.

The loading display adapts to the channel pane and terminal size, keeps the sidebar visible on wide terminals, and gives way to the destination channel's first complete frame. Startup selection is verified across cold, loaded and attached channels: the newest unattached channel for the folder wins.
