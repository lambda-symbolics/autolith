# Autolith 0.67.22

## Improvements

- Show pending, successful, and failed tool calls on their original fullscreen transcript entries, with errors beneath the invocation and arguments. Include background completions and restored history.
- Preserve authentication hints from provider responses and show bounded, credential-redacted diagnostics for provider failures. Thanks to @htayj for #183.
- Announce blocked, exhausted, and failed missions in the transcript, including startup exhaustion and controller stops without final prose. Thanks to @htayj for #185.

## Fixes

- Keep served tool output stable when only a cache retention estimate has elapsed, including same-provider resumes without a declared lifetime.
- Charge bounded child failures against their mission reservations and retain per-child accounting.
- Suppress delayed terminal notices after a mission is resumed, cleared, or replaced.
